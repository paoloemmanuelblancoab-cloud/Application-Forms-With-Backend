Get to know us

We're a Filipino mass media organization built around campus journalism education and media literacy — connecting student publications, training the next wave of campus reporters, and giving young Filipinos the tools to tell their communities' stories responsibly.

📣

What we stand for

Our advocacy

Good journalism doesn't start in a newsroom — it starts from initiative, with a student learning to ask the right questions. JourKnows PH exists to strengthen that starting point: training campus journalists in ethics and craft, and helping every Filipino to have access in Journalism.

Press Freedom Media Literacy Campus Journalism Education
💡

Who we are

Rooted in the campus press

JourKnows PH grew out of the campus journalism community itself, namely from "Campus Journalist Ako!"—student writers, editors, photographers, and designers who wanted a shared space to learn from each other. We run workshops, share resources, and spotlight the work coming out of publications across the country, because a stronger campus press today means a stronger press corps tomorrow.

Meet the desks

Every desk plays a different part in getting a story from idea to publication. Here's what each one does — and where you might belong.

📰

News

Covers national and community happenings on tight deadlines, getting the who, what, when, and where out first, and getting it right.

🏆

Sports

Chases the score and the story behind it, from national showdowns to local tournaments, and everything riding on the outcome.

💬

Opinion

Gives students a platform to argue, reflect, and take a stand — sharpening how they thinks and talk about what truly matters.

🖋️

Feature

Slows down to tell the human stories behind the headlines from profiles, narratives, and the details a straight news report skips.

🔬

Sci-Tech

Translates research, innovation, and campus tech developments into stories readers actually want to read.

📚

Literary

Curates poetry, fiction, and creative nonfiction for the publication's literary folio — a home for compelling narratives.

🎨

Cartoon

Illustrates campus life and current events through editorial cartoons and comic strips — humor and commentary in one frame.

🎙️

Broadcast

Brings the news to air and screen — anchoring, reporting, and producing video and audio segments for the publication.

✏️

Copyediting & Language Review

Guards grammar, clarity, and house style — the last checkpoint before anything goes to print or posts online.

🖼️

Graphics & Layout

Designs the visual identity of every issue — page layout, infographics, and the look readers recognize at a glance.

📸

Photojournalism

Captures the moments words can't quite hold — event coverage, portraits, and the images that anchor every story.

🎬

Video Editing

Cuts raw footage into polished segments — pacing, sound, and visuals that turn coverage into something people actually watch.

📱

Social Media Management

Runs the publication's online presence — scheduling posts, growing reach, and translating stories for feed-scrolling readers.
